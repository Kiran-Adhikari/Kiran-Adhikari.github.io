Bioinformatics Scientist

Kiran Adhikari

I build production genomics pipelines and machine learning models that turn raw sequencing data into decisions. Day to day, I run NGS workflows for RNA-seq, WGS, metagenomics, and DNA methylation on Nextflow, Argo, and Kubernetes, and apply deep learning to biomarker discovery in liver disease.

Focus

What I work on

Pipelines

Designing scalable, reproducible pipelines that process large-scale sequencing data, using Nextflow, Argo, Docker, and Kubernetes across HPC and cloud infrastructure.

Biomarkers

Applying multi-omics analysis and machine learning to bulk and single-cell transcriptomic data to find disease-associated gene signatures that hold up across patient cohorts.

Long-term goal

To become an independent scientist who integrates molecular and computational research to identify and validate therapeutic targets, advance drug discovery, and develop personalized treatments.
