Conference abstracts and posters

  • SLM 2026 A Deterministic Guideline-Grounded Large Language Fibrosis Triaging Model
    Adhikari K, Joshi D, Balasubramanian L, Badarkhe A, Saha S, Irali S, Saha S, Thirunagari S, Wong R, Alshammari M · Saudi Liver Meeting (SLM), 2026 · Poster
    Summary

    Summary

    A two-layer tool that triages liver fibrosis risk in steatotic liver disease from routine clinical and laboratory data. A deterministic layer computes established scores (FIB-4, APRI, NFS) and controls referral decisions, while a large language model, supported by retrieved guidelines and similar cases, estimates fibrosis stage. In 3,479 adults from NHANES, it detected F2+ fibrosis with 89.4% recall, 99.5% precision, and an AUC of 0.86.

    Conclusion

    The LLM-based triage model shows potential to identify at-risk patients earlier, guide specialist referral, and reduce unnecessary testing.

  • ICGM 2026 Multi-Modal Fusion Framework for Hepatocellular Carcinoma Prediction
    Adhikari K, Joshi D, Balasubramanian L, et al. · International Conference for Genomic Medicine (ICGM), 2026 · Poster
    Summary

    Summary

    A deep learning framework that combines bulk RNA-seq, contrast-enhanced CT scans, and clinical data to predict hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). Each data type is converted into a compact embedding, and the embeddings are fused to classify HCC stage, with the aim of improving early detection and survival prediction over single-modality approaches.

    Conclusion

    The framework offers a scalable way to integrate transcriptomic, imaging, and clinical data for HCC diagnosis and survival analysis, and can be adapted to other liver diseases.

  • ICGM 2026 Stacked Machine Learning Model for Gene Interactions Between Extra-Hepatic Comorbidities in MASLD
    Badarkhe A, Kaur P, Munikoti Srikantamurthy M, Pawar S, Adhikari K, et al. · International Conference for Genomic Medicine (ICGM), 2026
    Summary

    Summary

    A stacked machine learning model that links transcriptomic data from extra-hepatic comorbidities (obesity, diabetes, and cardiovascular disease) with the stages of MASLD progression. It identified a 23-gene biomarker, supported by literature and pathway analysis, that points to genes driving the interplay between these conditions and disease severity.

    Conclusion

    The pipeline provides a robust, scalable way to study how multiple extra-hepatic conditions interact in MASLD progression.

  • MHSRS 2025 Portable DNA Sequencing Technologies Supporting Far-Forward Operations
    Lar O, Clark J, McArdle J, Adhikari K, Allely E · Military Health System Research Symposium (MHSRS), 2025
    Summary

    Summary

    A comparison of Oxford Nanopore (MinION) and Illumina sequencing for field-deployable military use. Across whole SARS-CoV-2 genome sequencing and human variant detection, validated by Sanger sequencing, ONT reached 100% concordance with Illumina while being smaller, lighter, and USB-powered.

    Conclusion

    ONT matches Illumina's accuracy while being simpler, faster, and more portable, making it well suited to far-forward biosurveillance and field genomics.
