Jan 2025 – Present

Bioinformatics Engineer (Contract)

Karyon Bio · Mountain View, CA

Clinical AI and transcriptomics for liver disease, alongside my full-time role.

Details
Liver fibrosis triage (first-author poster, SLM 2026)
  • Co-developed a non-invasive fibrosis triage system that works from routine serum labs and returns a refer/clear decision for second-line testing.
  • Designed in three layers: deterministic clinical scores (FIB-4, APRI, NFS, a metabolic-evidence score), per-patient guideline retrieval so every call cites guideline text, and nearest-neighbour grounding followed by LLM staging.
  • Safety by design: the referral decision is written only by deterministic code, and automated tests enforce that the language model has no path to change it.
  • My components: the deterministic triage pathway, the metabolic-risk layer, the kNN model, the validation runs, and the API.
Population cohort engineering
  • Built the analysis-ready NHANES 2017–March 2020 dataset: 15,560 participants × 108 variables joined from ~28 CDC files, with derived clinical indices (FIB-4, APRI, NFS, HOMA-IR, eGFR, ASCVD risk) and elastography-based fibrosis stages.
  • Designed leak-free reference and test splits, kept the 2021–2023 cycle separate for independent validation, and harmonized NHANES with external cohorts from India and Iran.
Transcriptomics & multimodal ML

  • Ran a multi-cohort replication study of MASH liver transcriptomes from public GEO data, adding cell-type deconvolution and DNA methylation.
  • Analyzed single-cell and single-nucleus liver fibrosis data with pseudobulk DESeq2, testing robustness across 150 models (cell types × contrasts × covariates).
  • Contributed to the comorbidity transcriptomics model (ICGM 2026), an NIH grant proposal on foundational AI models for fatty liver disease biomarkers, and a NAFLD/NASH meta-analysis review.
